Leaf dynamically links its Kindle MTP helper to:

libmtp 1.1.23 — https://github.com/libmtp/libmtp/tree/v1.1.23
libusb 1.0.29 — https://github.com/libusb/libusb/tree/v1.0.29

The libraries are distributed under LGPL-family licenses. Their copyright
notices, authors, full license texts, and the exact unmodified source archives
are included alongside this notice in Contents/Resources/MTPLicenses.

To rebuild, copy build_mtp_dependencies.sh and both source archives to a local
directory. Place the archives under PREFIX/share/leaf-mtp, then run:
    bash build_mtp_dependencies.sh PREFIX arm64
Use x86_64 in place of arm64 for an Intel build. The compiler and SDK supplied
with Xcode and pkg-config/pkgconf are required. The script uses macOS 13.0 as the deployment target.
Rebuilt dynamic libraries can replace the copies in Contents/Frameworks.
Local replacements require updating load paths and re-signing the libraries,
helper, and application with an ad-hoc or your own code-signing identity.
Leaf's build_mtp_helper.sh performs relocation and nested signing in the repo.

You may modify these libraries and reverse engineer the linked helper to debug
your modifications as permitted by their licenses. Leaf does not impose a
restriction on those rights. No library source modifications are used here.
